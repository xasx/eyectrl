Proposed
Same six actions, same 120 DIP pitch, same tokens, no text on the faces. Drawn at 1:1 — look at a target to dwell it.
The shape is the target
The dwell target is a circle of radius Size/2, so the button is drawn as a disc. Everything you can see, you can hit; nothing you can hit is invisible.
Progress where the eye already is
A 4.8 px arc sweeping just inside the rim replaces the 4 px bottom bar. It surrounds the fixation point instead of sitting 60 px below it, and it never washes over the icon. The weight is 4% of the button — the same fraction the action menu and edge bar already draw.
Acquired, before committed
On entry the rim goes to 2 px #A5F3FC and the fill to #3A3A5E — you know you are on target before the timer means anything. IsHighlighted already exists on the view-model and is currently never bound.
Icons that read at a glance
The arrows glyph and the loop glyph do not read as "switch windows" and "reset adaptation". Drawn vector icons at 54 px carry the meaning on their own — and carry no font-coverage risk, which is what turned the old window emoji into tofu.
Mode needs its own icon
With no label, a background-colour swap is the only channel left, and it is not enough. Three distinct icons, tinted Manual / Auto / Off — they have to be told apart at 54 px, which is worth checking on the tracker.
Risk is visible and slower
Reset is ruled off from the row it can be overshot into and carries the warning hue. It keeps the standard 700 ms — spec 8.6 wants it reachable when adaptation has gone wrong — and a confirm step protects it instead. See Rethink 3.
The provider indicator moves out of the target row to a 27.6 px badge behind a rule: it is not dwellable, so it should not look like a target, and nothing keeps chrome out of the band today. Setting it twice currently appends a second one.
