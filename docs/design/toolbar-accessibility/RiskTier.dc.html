Keep the dwell at 700 ms. Spec §8.6 wants this reachable precisely when the adaptation has gone wrong and you are already struggling — making it slower would punish exactly the person it exists for. What the confirm buys is that no single mis-aim can destroy anything, at a cost of one extra 700 ms when you meant it.
Cancel is the bigger target (136 px against 104 px) and sits nearer the button you came from, so the cheap saccade is the safe one. Confirm carries the warning hue and is the one you have to reach for. The two confirm targets keep their words: this is a question, not a toolbar face, and the words are what make the answer unambiguous.
Two things to decide before building: whether looking away from the confirm counts as Keep it — it should — and how long it waits before deciding that for you.
The whole strip is also worth a pass for this: today Reset sits 24 px from Settings with nothing between them, which is the one adjacency where an overshoot is unrecoverable.
